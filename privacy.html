Privacy Policy

Chains of Chaos respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected while using the game.

Personal Information

The current version of Chains of Chaos does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect or store personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number

Anonymous Technical Information

The game or third-party services may collect anonymous technical information to help improve performance, stability, and compatibility. This may include:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Crash reports
  • Performance diagnostics

This information cannot reasonably be used to identify you personally.

Advertising

If advertisements are included in future versions of the game, third-party advertising providers may collect anonymous information according to their own privacy policies.

Future Online Features

Future updates may introduce optional features such as online multiplayer, player accounts, leaderboards, cloud saves, or game statistics. If these features are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated before they become available.

Children

This game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward young children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
