Privacy Policy

Cribbage of Chaos respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the game.

Information We Collect

The current version of Cribbage of Chaos does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address

Anonymous Data

The game or third-party services may collect anonymous technical information to help improve performance and stability, including:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Crash reports
  • Performance diagnostics

This information cannot reasonably be used to identify you personally.

Advertising

If advertisements are included in future versions of the game, third-party advertising providers may collect anonymous information according to their own privacy policies.

Future Online Features

Future versions of Cribbage of Chaos may include optional online features such as player accounts, multiplayer, leaderboards, cloud saves, or statistics. If these features are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated before they become available.

Children's Privacy

This game is intended for a general audience. It is not directed specifically toward children under the age required by applicable privacy laws.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
