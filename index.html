Chains of Chaos 500+ Downloads Milestone

Chains of Chaos has officially reached 500+ downloads on the Google Play Store.

This milestone reflects the continued support from players, testers, and the community. What started as a creative expansion of Northern Flights Disc Golf has grown into a full game experience enjoyed by players across multiple regions.

To celebrate, we’ve released a brand-new commercial showcasing gameplay, style, and the world of Chains of Chaos.

From platforming challenges to boss battles and unlockable music, development continues as we expand the game with new content and features.

Thank you to everyone who has downloaded and supported the game.

More updates and levels are on the way.

Our Mission Statement

Our mission is to ignite a passion for disc golf across Northern Canada by building accessible and sustainable courses, sponsoring emerging talent, and hosting dynamic tournaments. We strive to create inclusive recreational opportunities that bring communities together, promote healthy lifestyles, and celebrate the beauty of the outdoors.
